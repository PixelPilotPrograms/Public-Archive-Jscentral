Privacy policy

JS Central is a personal portfolio and experimental site. It does not currently offer accounts, purchases, subscriptions, contact forms, comments, or user-uploaded content.

Information collected

If you contact the site owner or public project channels, the sender address, message, and any information you include may be received so the message can be answered. Do not send passwords, financial information, government identifiers, or other sensitive information.

The site stores one preference in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme choice. This is not used to identify you and is not transmitted to JS Central.

Google advertising

This site loads Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, its advertising partners, and service providers may collect or receive information such as browser, device, network, approximate location, page activity, and advertising identifiers through cookies or similar technologies. They may use that information to deliver, limit, personalize, and measure ads, subject to your region and the choices presented to you. JS Central does not control Google's data practices.

You can manage Google's use of advertising personalization through Google Ads Settings. You can also manage cookies and permissions through your browser. Where required, Google's consent tools may ask for your choices before personalized advertising or related storage is used.

Cloudflare delivery, security, and statistics

This site is delivered and protected by Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical request information such as IP address, browser and device details, referring URL, requested page, date and time, country or region, and security signals to provide the site, detect abuse, protect against attacks, and maintain reliability. The site owner also uses Cloudflare statistics to understand traffic, performance, and aggregate visits. Cloudflare may use cookies or similar technologies where needed for security or service operation. Cloudflare's own privacy policy and configuration choices apply to its processing.

Advertising and Cloudflare statistics are third-party services. JS Central does not sell personal information or use a separate first-party analytics database. Information sent directly through contact channels is handled as described above.

Rights and requests

You may ask what personal information was received through contact channels, request correction or deletion of a conversation, or ask a privacy question by contacting the site owner through public channels. Requests may require reasonable verification. For data held by Google or Cloudflare, you may also need to contact that provider or use its privacy controls. If the site's data practices materially change, this page must be updated and any legally required consent controls must be added.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child sent personal information, contact the site owner through the public channels so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.
